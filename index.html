COMBAT TEST

Tap a friendly unit to select it, then tap anywhere on the battlefield to issue a routed move order. Persistent MOVE orders now survive local traffic and controlled re-pathing; repeated terrain taps keep the current selection locked unless you deliberately tap close to another friendly unit. Tap a hostile building/unit with the Aegis-X selected (or with no unit selected) to aim the main gun, then FIRE. Mobile input now treats up to 28 px of touch drift as a command tap before FREE CAM panning begins, keeps selected-unit switching intentionally tight, and uses terrain-ray fallback plus nearest-valid destination snapping so roads, trees, rocks, or tiny gaps cannot silently eat a command. Select a Field Harvester and tap near a Rich/Dense crystal field to mine it. Use FREE CAM to detach, one-finger drag to pan, and pinch to zoom; FOLLOW returns to the selected unit. With no unit selected, battlefield taps still aim the Aegis-X turret.
